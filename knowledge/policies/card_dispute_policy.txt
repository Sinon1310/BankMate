DOCUMENT: CARD DISPUTE HANDLING POLICY
DOCUMENT ID: POL-001
VERSION: 1.0

PURPOSE:
This document describes the illustrative process for
handling disputed card transactions.

DISPUTE INITIATION:
A customer who identifies an unauthorized or incorrect
card transaction should report it through the bank's
official customer support channel.

REQUIRED INFORMATION:
The customer should provide:
1. Customer ID.
2. Card or account reference.
3. Transaction date.
4. Transaction amount.
5. Merchant name.
6. Reason for raising the dispute.

INVESTIGATION:
The operations team should verify the transaction
details and review the available supporting evidence.

STATUS TRACKING:
Each dispute should receive a unique dispute reference
number. Its status should be tracked as Open,
Under Investigation, Resolved or Rejected.

RESOLUTION:
The outcome should be communicated to the customer
through an approved communication channel.

IMPORTANT:
These are fictional training procedures created for
the BankMate project. They are not actual bank policies.
